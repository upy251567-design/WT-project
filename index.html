Welcome to Our Department

Welcome to the Information Technology and Computer Science Department. Our department provides students with knowledge and practical skills in computer technology and programming.

About Department

The Information Technology and Computer Science departments focus on developing technical knowledge, programming skills, problem-solving abilities and practical knowledge among students.

Students learn subjects such as programming, web technology, database management, computer networks and software development.

Courses Offered

Information Technology

Learn programming, web development, databases, networking and software technologies.

Computer Science

Learn computer programming, algorithms, operating systems, databases and computer applications.
